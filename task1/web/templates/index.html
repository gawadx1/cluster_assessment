    Revenue definition

    Revenue is the sum of the clean revenue ledger. APP includes paid/shipped orders; ERP includes matched, valid, positive reconciled invoices; LEGACY includes parseable pharmacy documents, with credit documents retained as negative amounts. Detail lines are aggregated by invoice, the date window is inclusive, and canceled, pending, out-of-window, zero, or null records are excluded according to the documented rules in README.md.
